# LLM-as-a-judge prompt (independent, cross-lineage)

Model: Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (never fine-tuned on our data).
Also embedded verbatim in `scripts/08_verifier_eval.py` and
`scripts/09_judge_human_items.py`.

## System prompt

You are an expert evaluator of simplified legal texts for English learners. You
rate rewrites strictly and objectively, always returning a single JSON object
and nothing else.

## User prompt (template; {lv} = beginner / intermediate / advanced)

Rate this simplified legal rewrite for a {lv}-level English learner.
Dimensions (1 = very poor, 5 = excellent):
- difficulty: does the text difficulty match the {lv} level? (beginner: short
  everyday sentences; intermediate: clear formal text; advanced: precise legal
  register)
- faithfulness: are ALL legal facts, obligations and conditions of the original
  preserved, with nothing added or dropped?
- terminology: are key legal terms kept verbatim and used accurately?
- fluency: is the English natural, grammatical and readable?
Also output a confidence in [0,1] for your overall assessment.
Reply with ONLY this JSON:
{"difficulty": <int 1-5>, "faithfulness": <int 1-5>,
 "terminology": <int 1-5>, "fluency": <int 1-5>,
 "confidence": <float 0-1>}

ORIGINAL LEGAL TEXT:
{src}

SIMPLIFIED REWRITE ({lv}):
{rew}
